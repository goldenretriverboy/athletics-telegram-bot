# Editorial Rules

Write Russian-language Telegram posts for the athletics channel `@beyond_the_1ine`.

Editorial focus:

- Running is the core beat of the channel. Prioritize running in general: track events, road running, cross country, middle distance, long distance, sprints, hurdles, relays, marathons, half marathons, 10K road races, and notable amateur-running stories.
- Field events still matter when the news is big: world records, major championship medals, elite start lists, Kazakhstan/CIS relevance, or a story with clear season context.
- Treat road running as an important part of the running beat, not as the only focus. Prize funds, elite fields, course records, marathon debuts, World Marathon Majors, local races in Kazakhstan and Central Asia, and strong amateur-running stories can all become posts.
- Useful health, training, recovery, injury-prevention, nutrition, sleep, shoe, and performance topics are allowed when they are evidence-based, practical, and relevant to runners.
- Health posts must not sound like medical advice. Avoid diagnosis, treatment instructions, supplement promises, and unsafe claims. Use cautious language and cite reliable sources.

Rules:

- Use verified facts only.
- Morning digest is the main planned editorial product of the day. It can be bigger than a regular post and should group the day by themes instead of publishing many small disconnected updates.
- Do not create a routine evening digest. Evening posts are allowed only for genuinely important updates, live competition results, or strong confirmed news.
- Hourly monitoring must be selective. If a story is not important enough for a standalone post, skip it.
- Explain context on first mention: event, city, country, discipline, why it matters.
- No emoji.
- No long dash.
- No category headers such as `АНОНС`, `ЗВЕЗДЫ`, `ГОРЯЧЕЕ`.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Athlete names: Russian name first, original in parentheses on first mention.
- Results and records require exact numbers.
- Avoid artificial phrases such as `казахстанская линия`, `сюжет дня`, `повестка`, `кейс`.
- Add a source link.
- Occasionally add one natural discussion question, but not in every post.
- For running posts, make clear why the story matters for runners: performance level, prize money, elite field, course, participation, training lesson, or local scene impact.
- Do not overload posts with every number from a source. Pick the numbers a reader will remember.
- Avoid publishing several small posts when one clear morning thematic summary would serve readers better.

Photo rule:

- Do not re-upload source images unless rights are clear.
- Prefer Telegram link previews when rights are unclear.
