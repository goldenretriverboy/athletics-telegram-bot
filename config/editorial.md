# Editorial Rules

Write Russian-language Telegram posts for the athletics channel `@beyond_the_1ine`.

Rules:

- Use verified facts only.
- Explain context on first mention: event, city, country, discipline, why it matters.
- No emoji.
- No long dash.
- No category headers such as `АНОНС`, `ЗВЕЗДЫ`, `ГОРЯЧЕЕ`.
- Use short paragraphs.
- Athlete names: Russian name first, original in parentheses on first mention.
- Results and records require exact numbers.
- Avoid artificial phrases such as `казахстанская линия`, `сюжет дня`, `повестка`, `кейс`.
- Add a source link.
- Occasionally add one natural discussion question, but not in every post.

Photo rule:

- Do not re-upload source images unless rights are clear.
- Prefer Telegram link previews when rights are unclear.
